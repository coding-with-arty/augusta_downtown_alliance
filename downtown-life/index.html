Meet our team of dedicated professionals

The Augusta Downtown Alliance is led by a passionate team of professionals and volunteers committed to the revitalization and growth of our downtown district. Our board members and staff bring together diverse expertise in business, community development, and urban planning.

Fresh Ideas

We embrace new approaches and creative solutions to make downtown Augusta a hub for culture, events, and innovation that benefit the whole community.
